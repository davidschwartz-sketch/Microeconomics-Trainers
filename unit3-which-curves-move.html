Which Curves Move?

A change in fixed cost and a change in variable cost move different curves, in different ways — and that one distinction decides what happens to output, to profit and to the shutdown price.

The four curves. AFC = TFC ÷ Q AVC = TVC ÷ Q ATC = AVC + AFC MC = ΔTC ÷ ΔQ
Fixed cost appears in AFC and ATC; variable cost appears in AVC, ATC and MC. Read off which of those a change touches and the rest follows.
