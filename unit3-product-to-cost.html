Short-run tables only: labor is the one input that varies, and it is paid a constant wage, which is what makes MC = wage ÷ MPL and AVC = wage ÷ APL exact rather than approximate. Product figures print to one decimal place and costs to the cent, so dividing the printed wage by a printed cost gives the printed product figure. Real schedules are rarely this tidy; the relationships between the columns are the point, not the numbers themselves.
