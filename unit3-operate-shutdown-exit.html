Operate, Shut Down or Exit

The quantity is already settled — marginal cost meets the price and that fixes it. What is left is reading the rectangle it leaves behind, and deciding whether a firm losing money should keep going anyway. In the short run, surprisingly often, it should.

The chain. Profit = (P − ATC) × Q Short run: produce if P ≥ AVC Long run: stay if P ≥ ATC TFC = (ATC − AVC) × Q
Fixed costs are sunk in the short run: owed whether the firm produces or not, so they cannot tell it what to do next. The question is not “am I losing money?” but “does producing lose less than closing?” — and it does, as long as the price covers average variable cost, because everything above AVC is a contribution toward the fixed cost the firm owes anyway. In the long run nothing is sunk, so the bar rises from the bottom of AVC to the bottom of ATC.
