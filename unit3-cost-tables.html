Short-run tables only: one input is fixed, which is what makes fixed cost fixed. The quantities usually step in whole units, so marginal cost can be read straight off the totals; where they do not, the problem says so. Every figure has been chosen to come out to an exact number of cents. Real cost schedules are rarely this tidy; the relationships between the columns are the point, not the numbers themselves.
